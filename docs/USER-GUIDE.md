## 1. For Visitors (Public Guide)

### Browsing Courses

- Go to the **Courses** page from the top menu
- Browse through available courses by category (Cadet Preparation, Scholarship, Foundation, etc.)
- Click any course to see its full details including description, duration, class level, and price

### Enrolling (Applying for Admission)

To apply for admission, follow these 5 simple steps:

**Step 1 — Personal Information**
- Click "Apply Now" or go to the **Enrollment** page
- Enter your Full Name, Email, and Phone Number
- Click "Next"

**Step 2 — Course & Guardian Info**
- Select the course you want to join
- Enter your Guardian's Name and Contact Number
- Add your Address and any extra message
- Click "Next"

**Step 3 — Create Account**
- Create a password (remember this — you will use it to log in later)
- Confirm your password
- Click "Create Account"

**Step 4 — Verify Email**
- A 6-digit code will be sent to your email
- Enter the code on the screen
- Click "Verify", then "Next"

**Step 5 — Submit Application**
- Review all your details to make sure everything is correct
- Click "Submit Application"

### After Applying

- You will see a success screen with a checkmark animation
- The academy admin will review your application
- You will receive an email when your application is approved or rejected
- Once approved, you can log in with your email and password

### Checking Application Status

- Log in with your email and password
- If approved, you will be taken to the Student Dashboard
- If still pending, you will see a message asking you to wait
- If rejected, you will see the reason

### Other Pages

- **About** — Learn about the academy's mission and values
- **Notices** — View public announcements
- **Testimonials** — Read what students and parents say
- **Blog** — Read articles and updates
- **Gallery** — View photos from academy events
- **Team** — Meet the faculty and staff
- **Contact** — Send a message to the academy

## 2. For Students

### Logging In

1. Click **"Login"** in the top-right corner of the website
2. Enter your email and password (the one you created during enrollment)
3. Click "Sign In"

If your application has not been approved yet, you will see a message explaining that.

### Student Dashboard

After logging in, you will see your dashboard with:
- **Enrolled Courses** — How many courses you are taking
- **Overall Progress** — What percentage of course materials you have completed
- **Items Completed** — How many learning items you have finished
- **Total Items** — All materials available to you

You will also see quick access to your courses and the latest notices.

### My Courses

- Click **"My Courses"** in the left sidebar
- You will see all courses you are enrolled in with a progress bar
- Click any course to enter it and see its chapters (subcategories)

### Inside a Course

Each course is divided into **chapters**. Inside each chapter, you will find learning items:

- **Video** (blue icon) — Watch recorded lessons
- **PDF** (amber icon) — Read documents
- **Image** (purple icon) — View pictures

**To mark an item as completed:**
- Click the circle next to it — it will turn green with a checkmark

**To view an item:**
- Click it to open a preview window

### Taking Exams

- Click **"Exams"** in the left sidebar
- You will see exam categories with the number of questions
- Click a category to start the exam

**During an exam:**
- **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)** — Click the radio button to select your answer
- **Subjective Questions** — Type your answer in the text box
- When done, click **"Submit Exam"**

**After submitting:**
- MCQs are graded automatically
- You will see your score (correct answers / total questions)
- Pass mark is 40%
- You can review each question to see what you got right or wrong

### Viewing Notices

- Click **"Notices"** in the left sidebar
- See all announcements from the academy
- Notices are color-coded by category (Admission, Exam, Holiday, Event, Announcement)
- Click any notice to read its full content

### Profile & Settings

- Click **"Profile"** in the left sidebar
- View your information: name, class, student ID, email, phone, address, guardian details
- Click **"Edit Profile"** to update your name, phone, address, or guardian info
- Click your avatar image to upload a new profile picture

### Notifications (Bell Icon)

The bell icon in the top bar shows your notifications:

- **New Notice** — When the admin publishes a notice
- **New Course Material** — When the admin adds new content to your course

**How to use:**
- Click the bell to see recent notifications
- Unread items have a blue dot
- The number on the bell shows how many unread notifications you have
- Click "View" to go to the related page
- Click "Mark read" to dismiss an item
- Click "Mark all read" to clear all

### Signing Out

- Click your avatar in the top-right corner
- Click **"Sign Out"**

## 3. For Administrators

### Logging In (Admin Portal)

Admin login is a two-step process:

1. **Open the Admin Login** — Go to the website and either:
   - Press **Ctrl + Shift + K** on your keyboard, OR
   - Quickly tap the academy logo (top-left) 5 times
2. **Enter Passcode** — Type the admin passcode and click "Verify Passcode"
3. **Enter Credentials** — Type your admin email and password, then click "Sign In"

### Admin Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a quick overview:
- **Total Students** — How many student records exist
- **Active Courses** — How many courses are published
- **New Enrollments** — Recent applications waiting for review
- **Pending Notices** — Notices that have not been published yet

### Managing Students

- Go to **Students** in the sidebar
- **Add a student** — Click "Add Student" and fill in the details
- **Edit a student** — Click the pencil icon next to any student
- **Delete a student** — Click the trash icon
- **Search** — Type a name or email in the search box
- **Filter** — Show All, Active, or Inactive students

### Managing Enrollments

When someone applies through the public enrollment form, their application appears here:

**Statuses:**
- **Unverified** — Applied but has not verified their email yet
- **Pending** — Email verified, waiting for your review
- **Approved** — You accepted the application
- **Rejected** — You declined the application

**To approve an application:**
1. Click the eye icon to view the full application
2. Click **"Approve"** — this creates a student account and sends an approval email
3. The student can now log in

**To reject an application:**
1. Click the eye icon to view it
2. Click **"Reject"**
3. Enter a reason (the student will receive this in an email)

### Managing Courses

- Go to **Courses** in the sidebar
- **Add a course** — Click "Add Course" and fill in: title, description, duration, class level, category, price, image
- **Add content** — Click a course to open it, then add:
  - **Subcategories** (chapters/topics)
  - Inside each subcategory, add **Items** (videos, PDFs, or images)

When you publish a notice or add new course content, students automatically get a notification.

### Managing Exams

- Go to **Exams** in the sidebar
- **Exam Categories** — Add categories like "Mathematics", "English", etc.
- **Questions** — Inside each category, add:
  - **MCQ questions** — Multiple choice with 4 options
  - **Subjective questions** — Free-text answer with a model answer
- **View Results** — See how students performed
- **Print PDF** — Print question banks for physical use

### Publishing Notices

- Go to **Notices** in the sidebar
- Click **"Publish Notice"**
- Enter: Title, Content, Category, Author
- Click **"Save"**
- All students will receive a notification

### Contact Submissions

Messages from the public contact form appear here:
- Unread messages have a blue "New" indicator
- Click to expand and read the full message
- Mark as Read or Unread
- Delete spam or resolved messages

### Settings

- Go to **Settings** in the sidebar (5 tabs)

**Profile** — Update your name, avatar, or password

**Site Settings** — Change the academy name, tagline, description, address, app icon, contact info, social media links

**Landing Content** — Customize the public homepage (hero section, about section, stats, call-to-action, footer)

**Sections** — Turn landing page sections on/off (Hero, About, Courses, Notices, etc.)

**SEO & Features** — Set meta description, enable/disable blog, enable/disable maintenance mode

### Blog

- Go to **Blog** in the sidebar
- **Create Post** — Write articles with the rich text editor (bold, italic, images, etc.)
- Save as **Draft** or **Publish**
- Add tags to categorize posts

### Other Management Pages

- **FAQs** — Add, edit, or reorder frequently asked questions
- **Faculty** — Manage faculty/staff profiles
- **Categories** — Manage course and notice categories
- **Testimonials** — Add or remove student/parent testimonials
- **Gallery** — Upload and manage photos

## 4. Troubleshooting

### Login Problems

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Account not yet approved" | Your enrollment is still pending. Wait for the approval email. |
| "Invalid credentials" | Check your email and password. Use "Forgot Password" if needed. |
| "Access denied" | Make sure you are using the correct login page (students use the public Login button, admins use the secret modal) |
| Admin passcode not working | Contact the academy administrator |

### Enrollment Problems

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| No verification email | Check your spam folder. If still not found, contact the academy. |
| Verification code expired | Contact the academy for a new code. |
| Can't submit enrollment | Make sure all required fields are filled. Check your internet connection. |

### Account Problems

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Forgot password | Go to the Login page → Click "Forgot Password" → Enter your email → Check your inbox |
| Need to update email | Contact the academy admin |
| Account locked | Contact the academy admin |

### Need More Help?

- Use the **Contact** page on the website
- Call the academy phone number listed on the website
- Use the WhatsApp chat button on the website
